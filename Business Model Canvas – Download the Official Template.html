About the Business Model Canvas

See the whole business on one page

Why use the Business Model Canvas?

Describe a business model on one page
Nine blocks cover who you serve, what you offer, how you deliver it, what it costs, and what it earns. Fill it once for one business model, not for the whole company.

See how the pieces depend on each other
The right side is the market. The left side is what it takes to serve that market, with value propositions sitting between them. Move a customer segment and the channels, activities, and costs all have to move with it.

Treat it as a claim, not a plan
Cost structure and revenue streams only hold if the seven blocks above them are right. Date and version the canvas, then redraw it as evidence comes in.

The Business Model Canvas comes from the best-selling book Business Model Generation. It's the most used tool in the world for innovation and entrepreurship.
